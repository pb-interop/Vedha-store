About Us

Vedha Homemade Food Products

Run out of Chennai, VHFP bottles up the recipes of two kitchens — wholesome, millet-forward food made the slow way, for families who want tradition back on the table.

Our Mission

  • Profit is not our motto. We believe in spreading health and happiness, not just business.
  • To remind our traditional, healthy recipes to our younger generations.
  • To highlight the incredible health benefits of millets and natural ingredients through our products.

Our Vision

To build a healthier society by reviving age-old wholesome food habits and creating awareness about millets and traditional foods, thereby enabling everyone to lead a happy, healthy and long life.
